01
The problem

Open spaces leak cooling.

Cooling a bus stop is like cooling a kitchen with the freezer door open.

Wind carries cold air away within seconds. So we do not count cold air made. We count heat taken off people, per watt.

07
Requirement 4 · Effectiveness

Efficient is not effective.

Safety first. Then comfort. Then cost.

  1. Safe in of hot hours, or out.
  2. Comfortable in of hot hours (one tie rule: within point counts).
  3. Cheapest per comfortable person-hour.

The dehumidifier: a useful failure

Drier air should feel better. But condensing water releases heat, and the compressor adds more. The dry air drifts out; the heat stays.
